BIOGRAPHY

Cuts that Impact. Stories that Stuck.

I’m a 3rd year BSc Visual Communication student at St. Aloysius University with a passion for video editing and visual storytelling.

I specialize in film cuts, reels, and post-production, focusing on clean edits, smooth transitions, and cinematic impact.

Alongside video, I also work in graphic design, combining creativity and aesthetics to deliver engaging and meaningful visuals.

SKILLS & TOOLSET

The software, methods, and specialized workflows that power my editorial process.

01

THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE CUT

Prioritizing emotional truth and rhythm above all else. Every edit is a deliberate choice to advance the narrative, build psychological tension, and invisibly guide the viewer’s eye through the frame.

02

NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE & PACING

Structuring raw footage into a cohesive, compelling journey. Focusing on character arcs, spatial continuity, and temporal manipulation to ensure the emotional stakes dictate the rhythm of the film.

03

STORY-DRIVEN LOOK DEVELOPMENT

Utilizing color grading and compositing strictly in service of the narrative. Establishing mood and highlighting thematic subtext through intentional color theory and lighting principles to elevate the frame.

04

AURAL STORYTELLING & SOUNDSCAPING

Treating audio as a vital narrative device. Layering atmospheric soundscapes, precise dialogue pacing, and rhythmic sound design to anchor the viewer in the world and amplify the emotional impact of every scene.
